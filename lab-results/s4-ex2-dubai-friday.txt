    **There is no 1-hour slot this Friday (30 October) that falls inside working hours in all four cities.** The best possible slot still leaves 2 of the 4 offices outside their hours.
    
    I used the office hours in `office-hours.md` and the UTC offsets that apply on Friday: Madrid +1, New York −4 (US clocks change on 1 November), Dubai +4, Tokyo +9. In UTC, each office's Friday hours are:
    
    - **Madrid:** 08:00–13:00 and 14:00–17:00 (lunch is 13:00–14:00)
    - **New York:** 13:00–21:00
    - **Dubai:** 04:00–08:00 (on Fridays Dubai works only until 12:00 local time)
    - **Tokyo:** 01:00–10:00
    
    New York's hours never overlap with Tokyo's or Dubai's. Madrid opens at the same moment Dubai closes. So at any time on Friday, at most 2 offices are inside their hours.
    
    These are the three best compromise slots:
    
    | # | Madrid | New York | Tokyo | Dubai | Outside hours |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | 1 | 09:00–10:00 | 04:00–05:00 | 17:00–18:00 | 12:00–13:00 | New York, Dubai (1h after its Friday close) |
    | 2 | 08:00–09:00 | 03:00–04:00 | 16:00–17:00 | 11:00–12:00 | New York, Madrid (1h before opening) |
    | 3 | 15:00–16:00 | 10:00–11:00 | 23:00–00:00 | 18:00–19:00 | Tokyo, Dubai |
    
    - Slot 1 is in Madrid's first hour of the day. Slot 2 is in Dubai's last hour of the day (Dubai closes at 12:00 on Fridays).
    - I'd pick **slot 1** if New York can join asynchronously or with a recording. I'd pick **slot 3** if New York has to be live and Tokyo can take a late call.
    - Moving the call to Thursday doesn't solve it either, because New York and Tokyo never overlap. But on Thursday, Madrid, Tokyo and Dubai all overlap from 09:00 to 11:00 Madrid. Only New York would then be outside hours, at 04:00–06:00 New York.
